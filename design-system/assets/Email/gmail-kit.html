Gmail kit for the library address

Two pieces. The signature goes on every email automatically. The formal template is for invitations and announcements. Open this page in Chrome, press a Copy button, and paste into Gmail. Words in yellow brackets are placeholders to replace.

1. Signature

For everyday mail and replies. Set it up once.

Maktabat Aljamea (Sindh)
The Library of Aljamea-tus-Saifiyah, Karachi

  1. In Gmail, open Settings (the gear) → See all settings → General → Signature → Create new. Name it "Library".
  2. Click in the signature box and paste (Ctrl+V, or ⌘+V on a Mac).
  3. Put the cursor at the very start, press Enter once, go back up to the empty line, and click the Insert image icon → Upload → choose aljamea-logo-signature.png. Leave it at its original size.
  4. Replace the yellow placeholders. Select each one and retype it, so the yellow highlight goes too.
  5. Fix the two links: select "Search the catalogue", click the link icon, and enter the OPAC address. Do the same for the email address with mailto: in front.
  6. Under Signature defaults, choose "Library" for both new emails and on reply/forward. Tick "Insert signature before quoted text". Click Save changes at the bottom.

Staff sign their own first name above the signature, so readers know who answered. If each person sends from the address in their own Gmail ("Send mail as") rather than logging into one shared account, each of them sets the signature once.

  1. Open a new message in Gmail. In the compose toolbar, click the pen icon (Insert signature) and choose No signature. The template has its own footer.
  2. Paste into the message body.
  3. Click the dashed logo box, delete it, then click the Insert photo icon → Upload → aljamea-logo-blue.png, with Inline selected. Click the logo and choose Small if it comes in large.
  4. Replace every yellow placeholder, and link the button: select its text, click the link icon, paste the address.
  5. Send it to yourself first, and open it on a phone as well as a computer.

To reuse it: once the text is the generic version you want, go to Settings → Advanced → Templates → Enable. Then in the message, open the ⋮ menu → Templates → Save draft as template. Check once whether the logo survives inside the saved template. If it doesn't, keep the template without it and insert the logo each time.
