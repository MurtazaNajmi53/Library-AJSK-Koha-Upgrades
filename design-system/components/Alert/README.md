An inline message in the flow of the page. Provide a title, body, and the right role.

## Variants
- Info (`role="status"`): band fill, blue icon. Search suggestions, neutral notes.
- Notice (`aj-alert--notice`, `role="alert"` only if it appears after an action): copper border and icon. Overdue items, blocks on the account.

## Content
Say what happened and what to do next, with the action as a link. No apology, no exclamation marks.

## Tokens
`surface-band`, `border-strong`, `text-heading`, `text-accent`, `radius-md`, `space-3`, `space-5`.
