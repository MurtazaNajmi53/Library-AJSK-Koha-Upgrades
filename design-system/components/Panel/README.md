The white home-page card. Provide a heading, an optional "more" link, and the body.

## Anatomy
`surface-card`, 1px `border-subtle`, 3px `ornament` top rule, `radius-md`, `shadow-card`, 20px padding; head row with `h2` and link.

## Layout
Home panels sit in a grid with `space-6` gaps: new arrivals, member panel and news side by side from 992px, two columns from 768px, one column below. Add `.aj-stagger` to the grid for the once-per-visit entrance.

## Don't
Nest panels, or turn every block of content into a panel. Results, records and steps are not boxed.

## Tokens
`surface-card`, `border-subtle`, `ornament`, `shadow-card`, `radius-md`, `space-5`.
