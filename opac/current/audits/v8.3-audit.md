# OPAC v8.3: full audit of v8.2

14 Nov 2026, on the live catalogue, which runs v8.2 (OPACUserCSS_V8.2 and OPACUserJS_V8.2 are pasted). Signed in to Chrome as a reader. Nothing in Koha was changed, and no order was placed.

This file covers the six known issues in [`docs/v8.3-issues/README.md`](../../../docs/v8.3-issues/README.md), the eleven phone issues in [`v8.3-backlog.md`](v8.3-backlog.md), and everything new the audit found. The plan for v8.3 is built from it.

## Method

**Automated probe.** Every page below was loaded in a same-origin frame at 390, 768, 1024 and 1440px, and again right to left (`dir="rtl" lang="ar"`, as Koha has no Arabic interface installed) at 390 and 1440px: 108 page loads. On each it measured sideways page scroll, elements past the viewport edge, controls under 44px, text under 13px (14px for Arabic), text contrast under 4.5:1 (3:1 at 24px and up), controls without an accessible name, images without `alt`, and the gap under the search band.

Pages: home, results (English, with "Available now" on, Arabic), record (on the shelf, read in the library only, Arabic), Advanced search, Most popular, Your summary, Checkout history, Holds history, Lists (yours and public), Search history, the cart page, Personal details, Charges.

**Visual pass.** Phone (390), tablet (768, 1024) and desktop (1440) screenshots of results, Select titles, the record page in both directions, Holds history, the login page and the kiosk (both fetched signed-out, so the session wasn't touched), the Click & Collect dialog (opened, then closed), the cart pull-out and New arrivals. Parts E, G and I skip frames, so for those the frame check was switched off in a `srcdoc` copy of the page.

**Skills.** The checklists from `design-skills:design-critique` (Nielsen severity 0–4, used below), `design-skills:accessibility-audit`, `hzblj-skills:ui-surfaces`, `ui-interactions`, `ui-typography` and `ui-motion`, `frontend-design`, and the three cloned repositories: anthropics/skills (frontend-design), vercel-labs web-design-guidelines (rules from `web-interface-guidelines/command.md`) and ui-ux-pro-max (`quick-reference.md` §1–9). Where they disagree with the design system, the design system wins (sentence case, not Vercel's title case; 13/14px type floor).

**Severity** (Nielsen): 4 blocks a task, 3 major, 2 minor, 1 cosmetic.

**Not covered:** dark mode (the OPAC stylesheet carries light tokens only, a v5 decision), a real touch device, 360, 430 and 820px one by one, Lists with content, Charges with a charge, Suggestions, Messaging, self-registration and the 404 page.

## Results of the automated probe

- **Contrast:** no failures on any page, at any width, in either direction.
- **Unnamed controls:** only the cover slider dots (N4).
- **Sideways page scroll:** Holds history at 390px (55px LTR, 29px RTL) and the record page at 390px RTL (24px). Every other page fits.
- **Small text:** the New arrivals count in the header (12.8px, every page) and the Series line in result rows (12.24px).
- **Small targets:** Search history checkboxes (13×13px), Personal details fields (23–28px tall), cover slider dots (24px). Koha's inline title links and the DataTables sort labels were also flagged; both are text links inside larger targets and pass the WCAG 2.2 24px rule.

## The six known issues: root causes

| # | Issue | Root cause (verified) | Sev |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Relevance order wrong ("Dante and Pound" above "The age of AI") | **Koha/Zebra, not our code.** The same query gives the right order with no limit, and the wrong one as soon as `limit=available` is added, whether the URL comes from our Sort link, Koha's own form or a hand-typed URL. With the limit, Zebra stops ranking by relevance: "ai" alone lists "The age of AI" first among 281 titles, and with `limit=available` it lists "Dynamic Aikido" first among 274. `sort_by=relevance` makes no difference. An item type limit sometimes keeps the order and sometimes doesn't. Passing the item type as a search term (`idx=itype&q=BK`) instead of a `limit` keeps the relevance order, but availability can't be written as a search term. | 3 |
| 2 | Sticky bar breaks to two lines with a quick filter on | **Section 10d's flex budget.** From 1200px the band is `flex-wrap: wrap`, with the search form at `flex-basis: 22rem` and the results bar at its full content width. At 1440px with "Available now" on, the buttons grow (tick, "1 applied" badge): 352 + 12 + 979 = 1,343px needed, 1,329px available, so the bar wraps under the search. Without a filter it fits by a few pixels, so any longer label (Arabic, a chosen item type) breaks it too. | 3 |
| 3 | Arabic results with no title | **Koha's results XSLT returns nothing for these records.** The server HTML for the row has no title, author or availability, only the cover and Koha's action links, before our script runs. The record itself is fine: its MARC has a 245, the record page shows the title, and the row's COinS span (built from the same search record) carries title, author and date. Across 4 Arabic searches, all 39 affected records have **two or more copies, all "Read in library only (Click & Collect)"**; no record with one such copy, or with a mix, was affected. Likely trigger: the not-for-loan description, possibly the "&", when Koha groups two or more copies. Needs an admin-side test to confirm (plan, step A2). | 4 |
| 4 | "Tick titles to act on them" sits higher than the buttons | The count line (`p.aj-seltools__count`) inherits a 9px bottom padding from a Koha rule. The flex row centres its box, so its text sits 4px above the buttons' centre line. It is also 15px against the links' 14.4px. | 2 |
| 5 | Too much empty space, small text on results | At 1440px: the cover is 100×150px with only 8px from the card's edge, against 20px on the text side; title 16px, author and status 15px; the row is 209px tall for three lines of text, with a 943px title line and an empty middle before the actions. On a phone: cover 64×96px, and the two action rows (Click & Collect, then Add to cart and ⋯) take more height than cover and title together (backlog 8). | 2 |
| 6 | Result titles keep "/ James J. Wilhelm." | Koha's XSLT puts the statement of responsibility in `span.title_resp_stmt` inside the title link, after a " / " text node. Part J strips it on the record page; Part F2 doesn't on results. The record page's browser tab title still ends in " /" too (N11). | 2 |
