# OPAC v8.3: plan (for approval)

Built from [`v8.3-audit.md`](v8.3-audit.md), the six known issues and the phone backlog. Nothing here is built yet. Numbers in brackets point at the audit: K = known issue, B = backlog, N = new finding.

## Release mechanics

Per CLAUDE.md: every installable file under `opac/` moves from `_V8.2` to `_V8.3` (the masters and paste copies in `opac/current/`, `reports/new-arrivals_V8.3.sql`, the three `_V` files in `opac/click-and-collect/`); the v8.2 copies of files that change go to `opac/legacy/v8.2/`; each master gets a "What changed in v8.3" block; INSTALL.md gets a v8.3 section; `node opac/current/build.js` regenerates the paste copies. The kiosk (`sco/current/`) is versioned separately: its fix goes in the OPAC stylesheet's section 17, so SCOUserCSS_V1 stays as it is.

Expected paste for the library: OPACUserCSS and OPACUserJS only, as in v8.2.

## B. Results page: space and type (K5, B8, N1–N6)

Desktop and tablet (from 768px):
- Cover 112×168px with the same 20px inset as the text (was 100×150, 8px).
- Title 1.125rem (18px) DM Sans 600, author and status 1rem (16px), Series 0.875rem (14px, was 12.24px).
- Title and details capped at 60ch, so the line no longer runs 943px; the actions column fixed at 13rem.
- Row height follows the cover, not the action column, so rows tighten.

Phone (under 768px), one row of actions instead of two:
- Cover 80×120px (was 64×96).
- Actions: **[Click & Collect, the row's width] [cart icon 44px] [⋯ 44px]** on one 44px line. Add to cart becomes an icon button on phones, with its full name for screen readers ("Add *title* to cart") and the "In cart" state shown on the icon. About 60px less per card, so close to two results a screen instead of one and a half.
- "by" and the first author never split (`white-space: nowrap` on "by" plus the first name).

Everywhere: Series line 14px; slider dots get a 44px hit area and names ("Cover 1 of 2"); result count grouped (16,844) and the heading one size down on phones; header New arrivals count 13px.

**Select titles (N5).** Two lines at most on a phone: count and Done on the first, the actions in one sideways-scrolling row under it. Desktop: one line, as now.

## C. Phone and tablet layout (B1–B5, B9, N13)

- **B1 band.** Phone and tablet get the results page's one-row band on every page: field, search button, advanced search icon; no scope select (Advanced search has it). Desktop keeps the scope select on every page. Band on a phone: 219px → about 68px.
- **B4/B5 gap.** One rule: a set gap (`space-6`, 24px; `space-4` on phones) between the band and the first thing on every page.
- **B9 breadcrumbs.** Hidden on phones everywhere (the back button and header cover it). On tablet and desktop: kept on account pages and Lists, dropped on results (it claimed "Advanced search") and on the login page.
- **B2/N13 tables.** The DataTables top row lays out as one line: count at the start, search and More options at the end; on phones, search full width, More options beside it. The "Showing 1 to 11 of 11 entries" line is hidden when there's one page (Koha's "Showing 50 items. Show all items" stays).

## D. Account pages and navigation (B6, B7, B10, B11, N14, N15)

These need your call on the pattern; the recommended choice is first.

- **B10 side menu.** Phone: a "Your account: *Checkout history*" button at the top of the page opens the menu as a bottom sheet (the Click & Collect sheet's look). Tablet: the same button, opening a side drawer. Desktop: a sticky sidebar with its top on the heading's line and a set width. (Alternative: a sideways-scrolling row of tabs on phones.)
- **B7 tables on phones.** Each row becomes a card with its columns stacked and labelled, and the action (Renew, Remove, Place hold) shown as a button; no green "+". (Alternative: keep the "+" and restyle it as a labelled "Details" control.)
- **B6 account menu.** 8px between rows; "Clear" moves out of the menu (it stays on the Search history page).
- **B11 cart count.** A count on the cart icon's corner in `action`, a pill, hidden at 0, "9+" over 9, and a name of "Cart, 2 titles".
- **N14** Search history checkboxes get 44px label rows. **N15** Personal details fields: 46px like every other field.

## E. Smaller copy and content fixes

- B3 login email: a full-width "Email the library" button with the address as plain text under it, breaking only after "@" or ".".
- N17 login labels: "ITS number", "Password", no colons (set from Part A, which already relabels the login field).
- N19 cart: "Click & Collect 1 title" when only some can be ordered.
- N20 cart: the generated cover for titles without an image.
