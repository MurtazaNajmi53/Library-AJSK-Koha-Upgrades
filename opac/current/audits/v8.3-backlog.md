# OPAC v8.3: backlog from real-phone testing

13 Nov 2026. Reported from a real phone (iPhone, Safari, about 390px wide) against the live v8.2 install. Screenshots are in [`docs/screenshots/v8.3-phone/`](../../../docs/screenshots/v8.3-phone/).

The v8.2 phone audit ([`v8.2-phone-tablet.md`](v8.2-phone-tablet.md)) passed several of these screens at 390px in same-origin frames. A real phone found things the frames missed, so v8.3 should be checked on a device, not only in frames.

Nothing here is fixed yet. "Likely cause" is a first read of `OPACUserCSS_V8.2.css`, not a confirmed diagnosis.

## 1. Advanced search wraps to a second row

On a phone the band stacks: scope select ("Library catalog"), the query field, a full-width search button, then the advanced search (sliders) button alone on a fourth row. The band is about 450px tall before any content.

Likely cause: the phone one-row layout (`flex-wrap: nowrap`, scope hidden) is scoped to `body:is(#results, #opac-detail)` (around line 2702). Every other page gets Koha's stacked Bootstrap columns.

Open question: should every page get the results-page band (one row, no scope select), or should the home page keep the scope select because it's the main place people start a search?

## 2. Checkout and holds history: search field and "More options"

The DataTables search box sits under "Showing 1 to 2 of 2 entries", about half width. "More options" sits on its own row under it, left-aligned and not lined up with anything. The count line is indented compared with the heading and the box. The user says desktop has the same problem.

Also seen in the same screenshots, not in the report:
- the "Author" header shows a boxed focus/hover outline after tapping (`checkout-history-top.png`)
- the sort arrows overlap the end of "Author"
- the cover column shows empty beige boxes for every row

Likely cause: the generic DataTables rules (around line 3373) style the input and buttons but don't lay out the `.dt-container` top row (info, search, buttons).

## 3. Login: the email pill

The address `library_support.karachi@jameasaifiyah.edu` is too long for a pill at phone width, so it breaks mid-word ("jameasaifiya / h.edu") inside a rounded outline. A two-line pill reads as broken.

Options to decide between: a plain underlined link, a full-width button with a short label ("Email the library") and the address shown as text under it, or letting the address break only at `@` or `.`.

## 4. Record page: no space under the search band

The cover and title start right at the bottom edge of the sticky search band. The breadcrumb is hidden on phones on this page (a v8.2 choice), so nothing separates them.

Likely cause: the band's containers are set to `display: contents` (around line 2573), so the band's old bottom margin is gone, and the hidden breadcrumb took the only remaining gap with it.

## 5. Home page after login: same gap problem

The "New in the library" card touches the bottom border of the search band.

Likely cause: different rule from 4 (the `display: contents` rule only covers results and record), so check the home page's `main` top padding separately. One shared fix (a set gap under the band on every page) would cover 4 and 5 together.

## 6. Account menu: misclicks on phone

Lots of mis-taps in the account menu on a phone.

On paper every item already meets the 44px floor (`.dropdown-item` has `min-height: var(--size-target)`, around line 771). So "make the targets bigger" may not be the whole fix. Things in the screenshot that could cause mis-taps:
- the rows are exactly 44px with no gap between them, so a thumb on the line between two rows can hit either
- "Clear" (deletes your search history) shares a row with "Search history", the item most people want. It's the narrowest target in the menu and sits right at the thumb's reach
- the menu opens over the search band, so a tap just outside the menu lands on the search field or the scope select behind it

Question for the fix: which items were being hit by mistake? If it's "Clear", move it out of the menu (it already exists on the search history page) rather than padding everything.

## 7. Tables with a green "+" to open hidden columns

On a phone, DataTables' Responsive extension hides the columns that don't fit and puts a green "+" button at the end of each row. Tapping it opens a child row underneath (`most-popular.png` shows one open: just "Place hold" on a row of its own). It doesn't look like part of the design: the green isn't a brand colour, the button carries no label, and the useful action (Place hold, Remove, Renew) is hidden behind it.

Pages: search history, checkout history, holds history, most popular. Probably every OPAC table that uses Koha's responsive DataTables.

Likely cause: nothing in `OPACUserCSS_V8.2.css` styles DataTables Responsive (`.dtr-control`, `.dtr-details`) at all, so this is Koha's default look.

Direction to decide: on a phone, show each row as a card with every column stacked (no "+"), or keep the "+" and restyle it as a labelled control. Cards are what the results and holdings already do.

## 8. Search results: card space goes to the buttons, not the book

On a phone the cover is 64×96px (around line 6565) and the title column is what's left beside it. Click & Collect (full width, about 50px tall) and the Add to cart + ⋯ row under it take up more of the card than the cover and title together. A card is roughly 560px tall, so a phone shows about one and a half results per screen.

Questions before redesigning:
- What should a reader see first when scanning: cover and title, or the action? Most people scan many results and act on one, which argues for a bigger cover and title and lighter buttons.
- Does Click & Collect need to be a full-width filled button on every card, or only on the one the reader opens? A smaller pill on the card plus the full button on the record page would halve the card height.
- Is Add to cart used enough on a phone to keep a full button on every card, or could it move into ⋯?

## Spotted in the screenshots, not reported

- Most popular: the subheading reads "in the past months" with the number missing (should be "past 3 months"). Probably a Koha template string, not our CSS.
- Search history: the page has two delete controls ("Select searches to: Delete" in the toolbar and a "Remove selected searches" button), a stray "|" before "Select searches to:", and the queries show raw index syntax ("kw,wrdl: The, (homebranch: AJSK)").
- Search history: the disabled "Remove selected searches" button is pale pink with white text, which looks well under 4.5:1. Check if disabled states are exempt in the design system.
- Results: the breadcrumb wraps so "› Results of search for 'The'" sits alone on its own indented line.
- Header: the cart count is a green square badge next to the cart, not in the brand colours (`most-popular.png`, `account-menu.png`).

- Login footer: the rosette watermark ends in a hard-edged rectangle partway down the link list ("Authority search"), so the crop looks like a mistake rather than a deliberate bleed.
- Record page: "Foods" in the title has a highlighted box with an underline, probably search-term highlighting carried onto the record page. Check if that's wanted.
- Record page: the "Published" line reads "MUMBAI; 2005; 2019-04-02". The last date looks like a cataloguing date, not a publication date (a data issue, not CSS).
