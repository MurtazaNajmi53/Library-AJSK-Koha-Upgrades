# OPAC v8.2: site-wide simplicity and clarity audit

13 Nov 2026, on the live v8.1 OPAC in a signed-in Chrome, at 1440px and in a 390px frame. The audit used the checklists from three skill repositories. None of their scripts were run.

- `anthropics/skills`: frontend-design.
- `vercel-labs/agent-skills`: web-design-guidelines, with its rules taken from `vercel-labs/web-interface-guidelines/command.md`.
- `nextlevelbuilder/ui-ux-pro-max-skill`: ui-ux-pro-max `quick-reference.md` sections 1–9, plus the Pre-Delivery Checklist in `pro-rules.md`.

Where these differ from the house design system, the design system wins. Vercel's "Title Case for buttons" gives way to sentence case. The type floor is the system's own: 13px for Latin text and 14px for Arabic.

"Status" is the Gate 1 re-check against the v8.2 preview on the live catalogue. The swap was done with `tests/opac-harness/live-swap.js`, and Koha itself was not changed.
